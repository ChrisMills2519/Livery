Farmhouse interior assets
=========================

kenney_furniture_kit/
 140 GLB models from Kenney's "Furniture Kit" (2018, v1.0).
  https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
  License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). See kenney_furniture_kit_LICENSE.txt.
  Only the .glb files are kept; the .fbx/.obj/.dae/.stl/.mtl copies in the
  original archive are redundant for Godot.

kenney_food_kit/
  Tableware and food GLB models from Kenney's "Food Kit" (2.0, 2024).
  https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit
  License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). See kenney_food_kit_LICENSE.txt.
  Used for the dining table settings, the dresser plates, the fruit bowl and
  the kitchen worktop.
  Textures/colormap.png is the kit's own palette atlas, taken from the same
  archive (Models/GLB format/Textures/colormap.png). Every model in the kit
  references Textures/colormap.png, so the folder has to sit beside the GLBs;
  without it all 34 models import with a missing texture.

quaternius/
  House plants, small tables, chairs and bath accessories from Quaternius's
  "Ultimate House Interior Pack" (June 2020).
  https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatehomeinterior.html
  License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0).
  The round carpet models in the pack are authored as upright discs, so the
  scene uses Kenney's flat rugs for floors instead.

shapespark_textiles/
  Rugs, curtains, pillows and blankets from the Shapespark asset library.
  https://github.com/shapespark/shapespark-assets
  License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). See shapespark_LICENSE.txt.
  Each GLB keeps its own PNG/JPG texture beside it.

textures/
  wood_floor_diff_1k.jpg
    Poly Haven, "Wood Floor" (CC0 1.0).
    https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor
  painted_plaster_wall_diff_1k.jpg
    Poly Haven, "Painted Plaster Wall" (CC0 1.0).
    https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_plaster_wall
  1024x1024 diffuse maps, downloaded through the public files API
  (https://api.polyhaven.com/files/<asset>).

  The four detail maps below are neutral-luminance greys, not colour: each one
  sits at a 0.90 mean so StandardMaterial3D can multiply it over the furniture's
  own albedo colour and leave the farmhouse palette untouched. They are built
  from the CC0 sources above by tools/make_interior_detail.py, which also
  documents the processing; edit that script rather than the JPEGs.

  detail_wood_diff_512.jpg
    Poly Haven, "Angli Veneer" (CC0 1.0), 1k diffuse.
    https://polyhaven.com/a/angli_veneer
  detail_fabric_diff_512.jpg
    Poly Haven, "Book Pattern" (CC0 1.0), 1k colour map: coarse plain-weave
    cotton, reduced to luminance.
    https://polyhaven.com/a/book_pattern
  detail_stone_diff_512.jpg
    Derived from painted_plaster_wall_diff_1k.jpg above, at lower contrast.
  detail_metal_diff_256.jpg
    Derived from the Angli Veneer, high-passed and streaked horizontally so the
    kit's taps, handles and appliance fronts read as brushed metal.
